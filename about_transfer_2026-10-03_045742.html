About Nursing Notebook

A simple academic resource platform for nursing students

Why Nursing Notebook?

Nursing academic resources are often scattered across different websites, social media platforms, messaging groups, and personal collections.

Finding a particular question paper can therefore take unnecessary time.

Make useful nursing academic resources easier to find and access in one organized place.

Our purpose

Nursing Notebook aims to make academic resources easier to find and organize, starting with B.Sc. in Nursing.

A student-built project

Nursing Notebook is an independent project created by a nursing student with an interest in both nursing education and technology.

What started as a small hobby project is being developed gradually into a structured academic resource platform.
