Mission & Project Scope

Nursing Notebook is developed to streamline academic exam preparation for students enrolled in the B.Sc. in Nursing degree program affiliated with Rajshahi Medical University (RMU).

Accessing authentic past questions has traditionally been challenging due to scattered files and inconsistent archives. This project organizes verified examination question papers by academic year, curriculum sequence, subject code, and exam type.

Content Integrity & Open Access

All course codes, subject titles, and sequence numbers strictly follow the syllabus set by Rajshahi Medical University. No synthetic or unverified question papers are included.

This archive is strictly for educational, non-commercial purposes to aid nursing scholars, educators, and candidates in understanding examination patterns and question trends.
