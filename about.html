Our Objective

As a fellow nursing student, this platform was built with a single mission: to archive and provide previous years' question banks and study materials at zero cost for 1st Year, 2nd Year, and all prospective students across Bangladesh.

What You Will Find
  • Written and MCQ question papers, 2018 to 2025
  • Papers sorted by year and subject, with subject codes
  • Rajshahi Medical University (RMU) papers for B.Sc. in Nursing
  • A short guide to the BNMC 2024 exam system, with the official PDF
Quick Answers
Is it really free?

Yes. Every paper is free to view and always will be.

Why does a paper say "Coming soon"?

It has not been collected yet. It will appear once it is added.

Can I send a missing paper?

Yes. Message us on Telegram or email, and we will add it.

A link is not opening. What now?

Tell us on Telegram or Facebook, and we will fix it.
